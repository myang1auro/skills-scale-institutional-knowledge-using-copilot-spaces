# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Project Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Project Leads bridge technical teams and program management, providing technical expertise to project planning and decision-making. They ensure technical feasibility assessments inform project scope and drive architectural alignment across teams.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct technical feasibility assessments for proposed work
- Advise on architectural decisions and technology trade-offs
- Identify and communicate technical risks and dependencies
- Collaborate with Developers on technical design and estimation
- Ensure technical debt is tracked and addressed appropriately

### Goals
- Enable informed decision-making through technical insight
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Ensure architectural consistency across the program
- Accelerate delivery through early identification of technical constraints

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Partner on technical design, mentorship, and code quality standards
- **Product Managers**: Provide technical constraints and feasibility input to roadmap planning
- **Project Managers**: Contribute technical risk assessment and dependency mapping to project plans

### Decision-Making Authority
- Authority over technical approach and architectural decisions
- Escalates to engineering leadership on major technology strategy shifts
- Collaborates with Project Managers on scope changes driven by technical constraints

### Required Skills
- Deep technical expertise in core technology stack
- Experience with system design and architecture
- Strong communication skills to translate technical concepts
- Project/program management understanding

### Typical Communication
- Technical design review meetings
- Architecture decision records (ADRs)
- Risk and dependency assessments with Project Managers
- Estimation and feasibility workshops with Developers

---

## Stakeholder Relationship Manager

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Relationship Managers own communication with external stakeholders, customers, and organizational leadership. They gather requirements, manage expectations, and ensure alignment between stakeholder needs and project delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct stakeholder interviews and requirements gathering
- Maintain stakeholder engagement throughout project lifecycle
- Translate stakeholder feedback into actionable requirements
- Manage stakeholder expectations and communicate progress transparently
- Identify and escalate stakeholder concerns and business alignment issues

### Goals
- Ensure customer and stakeholder satisfaction
- Align project outcomes with business objectives
- Reduce scope creep through clear requirements and expectation management
- Build strong stakeholder relationships and trust

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Product Managers**: Share stakeholder insights and feedback to inform prioritization
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate stakeholder communications and expectations management
- **Developers**: Communicate requirements clarity and acceptance criteria from stakeholders

### Decision-Making Authority
- Authority to represent stakeholder voice in prioritization discussions
- Escalates critical stakeholder concerns to Project and Product Managers
- Collaborates on trade-off decisions affecting stakeholder value

### Required Skills
- Strong communication and interpersonal skills
- Requirements gathering and analysis expertise
- Business acumen and understanding of customer context
- Conflict resolution and negotiation abilities

### Typical Communication
- Regular stakeholder check-ins and status updates
- Requirements documentation and clarification sessions
- Feedback synthesis and impact analysis
- Executive briefings and business alignment reviews

---

## Quality Assurance Owner

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Owners define quality standards, acceptance criteria frameworks, and testing strategies. They oversee validation workflows and ensure delivered products meet quality expectations and regulatory requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards and acceptance criteria frameworks
- Design and oversee testing strategies (unit, integration, UAT, etc.)
- Manage test planning and execution
- Identify and track quality issues and defects
- Ensure compliance with regulatory and organizational quality requirements

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality products that meet or exceed stakeholder expectations
- Reduce defects reaching production
- Build confidence in product quality across the organization
- Optimize testing efficiency and cost

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Define acceptance criteria, collaborate on quality standards, review test coverage
- **Product Managers**: Ensure acceptance criteria align with product vision and user needs
- **Project Managers**: Communicate quality risks, testing timeline, and resource needs

### Decision-Making Authority
- Authority over quality standards and acceptance criteria
- Can block release if quality thresholds not met
- Escalates quality risks and compliance issues to Project Managers and leadership

### Required Skills
- Quality assurance and testing expertise
- Knowledge of testing frameworks and automation
- Regulatory compliance understanding (if applicable)
- Analytical and problem-solving skills

### Typical Communication
- Test strategy and test plan documentation
- Quality metrics and defect tracking
- Acceptance criteria and quality standard reviews
- Release readiness assessments

---

## Release Coordinator

### Role Summary
Release Coordinators manage the release planning, deployment scheduling, and execution across multiple teams. They ensure smooth, coordinated releases with minimal disruption and clear communication across organizational boundaries.

### Responsibilities
- Plan release schedules and coordinate across teams
- Manage release documentation and deployment procedures
- Orchestrate deployment activities and rollback procedures
- Communicate release status and impact across the organization
- Track and resolve release-blocking issues and dependencies

### Goals
- Execute reliable, predictable releases
- Minimize deployment risk and downtime
- Reduce time-to-market for features and fixes
- Maintain clear communication and coordination across teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate release timing with project delivery plans
- **Developers**: Communicate deployment requirements and deployment windows
- **Quality Assurance Owners**: Ensure quality sign-off before release approval

### Decision-Making Authority
- Authority over release scheduling and deployment procedures
- Can delay or halt release if readiness criteria not met
- Escalates cross-team coordination issues to Program or Project leadership

### Required Skills
- Release management and deployment expertise
- Process coordination and project management
- Communication and stakeholder management
- Technical understanding of deployment infrastructure

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and kick-off meetings
- Deployment procedures and runbooks
- Status updates and release notes
- Post-release retrospectives and incident reviews

---

## Process Improvement Facilitator

### Role Summary
Process Improvement Facilitators lead retrospectives, collect team feedback, and drive continuous refinement of project management and delivery processes. They are distinct from Project Managers, focusing specifically on optimizing how work is done rather than coordinating individual projects.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate retrospectives and feedback sessions
- Collect and synthesize lessons learned across projects
- Identify process improvement opportunities and bottlenecks
- Develop and implement process improvements
- Track effectiveness of process changes and iterate

### Goals
- Continuously improve team efficiency and effectiveness
- Reduce process waste and cycle time
- Build a culture of continuous improvement
- Increase team satisfaction and engagement

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: Partner on retrospective facilitation and process implementation
- **Developers**: Gather feedback on process pain points and effectiveness
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate on prioritization of improvements affecting cross-team workflows

### Decision-Making Authority
- Authority to recommend and implement process changes
- Escalates process changes affecting multiple teams or programs to leadership
- Collaborates with Project Managers and team leads on implementation

### Required Skills
- Facilitation and group dynamics expertise
- Process analysis and optimization skills
- Change management understanding
- Data analysis for measuring process effectiveness

### Typical Communication
- Retrospective facilitation and documentation
- Process improvement proposals and recommendations
- Metrics and effectiveness dashboards
- Training and change management communications
